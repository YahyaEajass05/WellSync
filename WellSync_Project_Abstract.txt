WELLSYNC: AI-POWERED MENTAL WELLNESS AND ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE PREDICTION SYSTEM

================================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================================

WellSync is an innovative digital health platform that leverages advanced artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms to predict, monitor, and improve the mental wellness and academic performance of students in educational institutions. The system addresses the growing concern of mental health challenges, digital addiction, and academic stress among students by providing data-driven insights, personalized recommendations, and proactive intervention strategies. Built with a modern full-stack architecture, WellSync integrates cutting-edge machine learning models, real-time analytics, automated notification systems, and comprehensive reporting to create a holistic wellness ecosystem for educational communities.

The proliferation of digital devices and increasing academic pressure in modern educational environments have created unprecedented challenges for student well-being. WellSync takes a proactive, predictive approach by continuously monitoring behavioral patterns, lifestyle factors, and digital habits to identify at-risk students before critical situations develop. The platform empowers educational institutions, counselors, and students themselves with actionable intelligence to foster healthier habits, reduce stress levels, and optimize academic outcomes.

At its core, WellSync employs three distinct artificial intelligence prediction models, each meticulously designed using advanced ensemble learning techniques. The Mental Wellness Prediction Model analyzes comprehensive lifestyle data including age, screen time duration, sleep quality, physical activity levels, social media usage patterns, and academic workload to generate a mental wellness score on a 0-100 scale. The Stress Level Prediction Model focuses specifically on quantifying student stress on a 0-10 scale by examining stress-inducing and stress-mitigating factors including sleep duration, exercise frequency, and coping mechanisms. The Academic Impact Prediction Model analyzes the correlation between digital habits, particularly social media usage, and academic performance, predicting addiction scores and their impact on a 0-9 risk scale.

These models utilize sophisticated algorithms including Random Forest Regressors, Gradient Boosting Machines, Extra Trees Regressors, and advanced ensemble methods such as Voting and Stacking Regressors. Through rigorous hyperparameter tuning using GridSearchCV and RandomizedSearchCV techniques, combined with 10-fold cross-validation, the models achieve exceptional predictive accuracy with R² scores exceeding 0.85 and low mean absolute error values. Feature importance analysis identifies critical risk factors, enabling targeted interventions and personalized recommendations.

The technical architecture demonstrates sophisticated software engineering practices. The backend infrastructure is built using Node.js with Express.js framework, providing a robust, scalable RESTful API architecture. The system implements comprehensive security measures including JWT-based authentication, bcrypt password hashing, Helmet.js security headers, MongoDB sanitization, and multi-tier rate limiting. The database layer utilizes MongoDB with Mongoose ODM, featuring carefully designed schemas for users, predictions, mental health assessments, wellness profiles, and analytics. The artificial intelligence service operates as an independent Python-based microservice using FastAPI framework, exposing RESTful endpoints with automatic OpenAPI documentation. This microservices architecture allows independent scaling and maintenance of AI components.

WellSync's notification and engagement system represents a critical component of its preventive health approach. The intelligent email service, powered by Nodemailer, delivers beautifully designed, responsive HTML emails with advanced CSS animations. The system sends six distinct email types: welcome emails with verification codes, email verifications, password resets, comprehensive prediction reports with PDF attachments, account activation notifications, and automated weekly wellness updates. Prediction report emails feature dynamic scoring displays with animated progress bars, color-coded risk assessments, personalized recommendations with evidence-based tips, and professionally generated PDF attachments containing comprehensive analysis and visualizations.

The weekly wellness update system operates on an automated schedule using node-cron, analyzing each user's prediction history over the previous 30 days. The system calculates statistical averages, identifies trends, and generates personalized, priority-ranked recommendations intelligently categorized into high, medium, and low priority actions. The recommendation engine draws from a comprehensive library of evidence-based interventions covering sleep hygiene, stress management, digital detox strategies, physical activity, and academic success techniques.

The model training infrastructure implements a rigorous seven-stage pipeline: data loading and exploration, preprocessing and feature engineering, train-test splitting, baseline model comparison across multiple algorithms, hyperparameter tuning, ensemble model creation, and comprehensive evaluation using R² score, MAE, RMSE, and MAPE metrics. All trained models are serialized using joblib for efficient inference with response times under 200 milliseconds. The system generates comprehensive visualizations including correlation heatmaps, feature importance charts, prediction plots, and residual analysis.

WellSync represents a comprehensive ecosystem for promoting student wellness through technology. By combining predictive analytics, personalized interventions, automated engagement, and institutional insights, the platform addresses the multifaceted challenges of modern student life. The system's evidence-based approach ensures recommendations are scientifically valid and practically applicable, demonstrating that artificial intelligence, when thoughtfully designed and ethically implemented, can be a powerful force for improving lives and fostering healthier educational communities.
