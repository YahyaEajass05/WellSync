WELLSYNC: AN AI-POWERED MENTAL WELLNESS AND ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE
PREDICTION SYSTEM FOR STUDENTS IN EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS
================================================================================

ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The digital mental health landscape, while rapidly evolving, remains characterized by fragmented and reactive solutions that fail to address the growing and complex needs of students in educational institutions. Rising levels of academic stress, digital addiction, poor sleep quality, and excessive social media consumption have created a silent mental health crisis among the student population — yet most existing platforms offer generalized advice without personalized, data-driven insights. This project identified these critical shortcomings through a comprehensive analysis of existing health platforms, academic research, and real-world student behavioral patterns, revealing a clear demand for an intelligent, integrated, and proactive mental wellness ecosystem tailored specifically to students.

To bridge these gaps, WellSync is proposed as the design and development of a comprehensive, AI-driven mental wellness and academic performance prediction platform. WellSync combines several key functionalities into a single, unified ecosystem:

   •  Mental Wellness Prediction Model that analyzes lifestyle factors including screen time, sleep quality, physical activity, and social media usage to generate a personalized wellness score on a 0–100 scale.
   •  Stress Level Classification Model that intelligently categorizes student stress into four levels — Low, Moderate, High, and Very High — enabling timely and targeted interventions.
   •  Academic Impact Prediction Model that assesses the risk of digital addiction and its direct influence on academic performance and productivity.
   •  Smart Notification and Reporting System that delivers personalized HTML email reports, automated weekly wellness summaries, and PDF-attached prediction reports to keep students and administrators informed.
   •  Role-Based Access Control with secure JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, rate limiting, and MongoDB query sanitization to ensure data privacy and platform integrity.
   •  Interactive Admin Dashboard that provides administrators with comprehensive analytics, user management tools, system-wide statistics, and model performance monitoring.

Developed using an Agile methodology, WellSync employs a modern three-tier architecture comprising a Next.js 14 TypeScript frontend, a Node.js and Express.js RESTful backend, and a Python FastAPI AI service. The machine learning models are built using advanced ensemble learning techniques — including Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and Stacking Ensemble algorithms — trained on real-world student behavioral datasets and achieving R² scores exceeding 0.85 through rigorous hyperparameter tuning and cross-validation. Custom-trained models are hosted locally to ensure full data privacy and control, while edge-optimized processing ensures consistent performance under varying server conditions.

Key differentiators of WellSync from existing solutions include its focus on holistic student wellness through multi-dimensional AI predictions, a proactive engagement system powered by automated cron-based email scheduling, an inclusive and accessible user interface with responsive design and dark mode support, and a robust administrative ecosystem for institutional-level monitoring and decision-making. The platform concludes that while technical and ethical challenges exist — including continuous model retraining, data privacy compliance, and the sensitivity of mental health data — WellSync's feasibility and impact potential are exceptionally high. It represents a scalable, low-cost, and high-impact solution with the power to democratize access to mental health insights, reduce academic dropout risks, and provide students and institutions with a more holistic, equitable, and intelligent approach to student well-being.
